The SDK draws traffic in five ways, each owned by one module. The playground below switches flow and incidents between presets:

<GuideDemo demo="traffic-config-playground" />

| You need… | Use | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Live speed on every road | [`TrafficFlowModule`](./traffic-flow.mdx) | the style's flow tiles |
| Live and planned incidents across the map, filtered | [`TrafficIncidentsModule`](./traffic-incidents.mdx) | the style's incident tiles |
| Exactly the incidents you fetched, with focus | [`TrafficIncidentDetailsModule`](./traffic-incident-details.mdx) | a [`trafficIncidentDetails`](../services/traffic/traffic-incident-details.mdx) response |
| The incidents on one route | [`RoutingModule`](./routes-sections.mdx) | the route's `traffic` sections |
| Historical speed and congestion over a region | [`TrafficAreaAnalyticsModule`](./traffic-area-analytics-module.mdx) | a [`trafficAreaAnalytics`](../services/traffic/traffic-area-analytics.mdx) response |

The flow and incident tiles are [style parts](./styles.mdx#available-style-parts), hidden until a module shows them.

## Traffic incidents

[`TrafficIncidentsModule`](./traffic-incidents.mdx) shows the live incidents of the whole map, such as accidents, closures, roadworks and weather, as lines and category icons. You filter them by category, severity, delay and road type, and bring in the [planned](./traffic-incidents.mdx#planned-incidents) ones too. No request is needed.

## Traffic flow

[`TrafficFlowModule`](./traffic-flow.mdx) colours every road by its speed relative to free flow, from free-flowing to stationary, with closed roads outlined.

## Traffic flow as data

Orbis flow comes as vector tiles only, so you can read it only with `TrafficFlowModule.getRenderedFeatures()` over the current viewport. [What the flow data can and cannot tell you](./traffic-flow.mdx#what-the-flow-data-can-and-cannot-tell-you) lists what each segment carries. Historical speed and congestion over an area come from [Traffic Area Analytics](../services/traffic/traffic-area-analytics.mdx).

## Traffic incident details

[`TrafficIncidentDetailsModule`](./traffic-incident-details.mdx) draws exactly the incidents a [`trafficIncidentDetails`](../services/traffic/traffic-incident-details.mdx) call returned, and highlights a subset with `setFocus`. Use it if your app also needs the typed incidents, for a list, a popup or a route.

## Traffic along routes

[`RoutingModule`](./routes-sections.mdx) draws the incidents of a calculated route as its `traffic` sections, in the [incident colours](./traffic-incidents.mdx#appearance).

## Traffic area analytics

[`TrafficAreaAnalyticsModule`](./traffic-area-analytics-module.mdx) draws a region's historical traffic as hexagon or square grids, flat or extruded, or as a heatmap, coloured by congestion, speed, travel time or network length.
