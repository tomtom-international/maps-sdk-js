The [Styling Foundations module](./styling.mdx)'s `view` knobs own the map-level MapLibre state that a style switch would otherwise reset — projection and sky — so a globe stays a globe across `setStyle`. They are [knobs](./styling.mdx#knobs) like any other: `setKnob`, `getKnob`, `resetKnob` and `getConfig()` carry them, and they work on any style, custom ones included.

## Projection and sky

```javascript
import { setKnob, StylingFoundationsModule, stylingFoundationsKnobCatalogue } from '@tomtom-org/maps-sdk/map';

const styling = await StylingFoundationsModule.get(map);
setKnob(styling, stylingFoundationsKnobCatalogue, 'view.projection', 'globe');
setKnob(styling, stylingFoundationsKnobCatalogue, 'view.sky', true); // without it, a globe has no atmosphere
```

- **The `globe` [preset](./styling.mdx#presets)** sets both in one call.
- **3D terrain is not a knob**: it needs the elevation style part loaded first, which [`TerrainModule`](./terrain.mdx#3d-elevation) does for you.

## Sky colours

`view.skyColor` and `view.horizonColor` colour the sky. Left unset, they take:

- **The style's own `sky`**, where the style declares one;
- **otherwise the style's light/dark theme**: a daylit blue with a white horizon over a light style, a night blue with a dim horizon over a dark one.

## The colour behind the map

`view.spaceColor` is the colour **behind** the map — what a globe leaves visible around the planet, where the map canvas is transparent.

- **Your page's background wins**: where the page already gives the map container a background, that colour is the knob's default and what a reset returns to.
- **Otherwise it follows the style theme** — white on a light style, the night sky's blue on a dark one — and is re-applied after a `setStyle`, so a light-to-dark switch carries it.

```javascript
setKnob(styling, stylingFoundationsKnobCatalogue, 'view.spaceColor', '#05070d');
```

## What to expect

- **The globe flattens to Mercator** between zoom 11 and 12, by design.
- **Fog over a tilted view** starts at pitch 60, which is also MapLibre's default `maxPitch`: raise `maxPitch` on the map to see it.

The demo above zooms a globe into 3D terrain; the [3D terrain playground](../../examples/terrain-playground) keeps a tilted camera, its sky and its relief across a style switch. The map effects plugin's [depth of field](../plugins/map-effects-depth-of-field.mdx) holds one distance of a tilted view sharp and defocuses the rest.
