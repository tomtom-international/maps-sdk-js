The [Routing module](./routes.mdx) draws routes in its own look. `color`, `widthPreset` and `opacity` style the whole route,
`waypoints.sizePreset` and `waypoints.label` the waypoints, `summaryBubbles` the bubbles, and `layers` each layer. You can try them all on the
route style playground. Styling knobs reach route layers only through the
[accent](./styling-map-colors.mdx#the-accent-what-a-theme-reaches-beyond-the-base-map), which a route without a `color`
defaults to, the label and halo colours its [summary bubbles](#summary-bubbles) take, and the delay colours of its
[traffic sections](./routes-sections.mdx#layer-overrides).

## Colour, width and waypoint size

```javascript
import { RoutingModule } from '@tomtom-org/maps-sdk/map';

const routingModule = await RoutingModule.create(map, {
    color: '#DF1B12',        // the route line, its outline (a darker shade) and the waypoint pins
    widthPreset: 'l',        // 's' | 'm' | 'l'
    widthFactor: 1.2,        // multiplies the preset, 0.5 to 2
    opacity: 0.7,            // the route lines and their outlines, 0 to 1
    waypoints: {
        sizePreset: 's',     // 's' | 'm' | 'l'
        sizeFactor: 1.5,     // multiplies the preset, 0.5 to 2
    },
});
```

- A factor multiplies its preset instead of replacing it, and is clamped to its `range` in
  `routingKnobCatalogue`, which `knobEntryOf(routingKnobCatalogue, 'widthFactor')` reads.
- `opacity` fades the route lines and their outlines, selected and deselected. Each
  [section](./routes-sections.mdx) keeps its own `sections.<type>.opacity`, and the waypoints, bubbles and
  instruction lines stay opaque.
- The instruction arrows keep one size whatever the `widthPreset`, and follow only `widthFactor`.
- The [stop marks](./routes.mdx#stop-marks) scale with their pins, by preset and factor, and stay
  centred in the pin head at every zoom.
- The waypoint labels follow the map's light or dark theme, like the labels of places and geometries, with
  dark text on a white halo on a light map and the reverse on a dark one. `waypoints.label` sets their colour,
  halo and size, as `label` does for [places](./places-styling.mdx#label-styling). [Waypoints](./routes.mdx#pins-per-role)
  covers pins per role, your own labels and icons per waypoint.

Without a `color`, routes take the map's
[accent](./styling-map-colors.mdx#the-accent-what-a-theme-reaches-beyond-the-base-map) when a theme sets one, or
the SDK's route blue otherwise:

```javascript
const brandedRoutes = await RoutingModule.create(map, { color: '#DF1B12' }); // stays red through any theme
const themedRoutes = await RoutingModule.create(map); // follows map.mapColors.accent
```

## Summary bubbles

Each route carries a bubble with its duration, distance and traffic delay. `summaryBubbles` decides what it says,
which routes carry one, where it sits, how it looks and at which zooms it appears. You can try each setting on the
summary bubbles playground:

<GuideDemo demo="route-summary-bubbles-playground" />

```javascript
const routingModule = await RoutingModule.create(map, {
    summaryBubbles: {
        fields: ['duration', 'trafficDelay'], // drops the distance
        minZoom: 8,                           // hidden while the whole country is in view
        color: '#1A2024',                     // the bubble, deselected routes a shade darker
        label: { size: 14, typeface: 'serif' },
    },
});
```

- `fields` lists `duration`, `distance`, `durationDifference` and `trafficDelay`. The first field a route carries
  is in bold and the others follow it muted. The traffic delay takes its own line, and only on routes with a
  delay.
- `durationDifference` times each other route against the selected one, such as `+6 min`. The selected route
  shows none, and neither does a route whose duration rounds to the same minute.
- A bubble with nothing to say isn't drawn. `['trafficDelay']` shows bubbles only on delayed routes,
  `['durationDifference']` only on the routes you can switch to, and `[]` shows none.
- By default, the bubble follows the map. Once a [theme](./styling-map-colors.mdx) sets the `labelHalo` colour, the
  bubble takes it, lightened to stand off the land, and once it sets `label`, the text takes that. Without them,
  the bubble is white on a light style and dark grey on a dark one.
- The text takes whichever of dark or light reads on the bubble, including a `color` of your own, and
  `label.color` overrides the selected route's text.
- `label.size`, `label.opacity`, `label.typeface` and `label.customTypeface` work as they do for
  [waypoint labels](./styling-typeface.mdx#your-routes-places-and-geometries).

### Which routes, and where

Alternatives often share most of their way, so each bubble sits on its route's longest
[distinct section](../core/utilities/routes.mdx#getdistinctsections), a stretch of road the route has to
itself, where the other bubbles can't hide it. `routes` picks which routes carry one, to keep them out of the way of a
panel or popup of your own:

```javascript
routingModule.updateConfig({
    summaryBubbles: {
        routes: 'alternatives',  // the selected route's trip is in your own panel
        fields: ['duration', 'durationDifference'],
    },
});
```

- The first route's bubble sits halfway along its longest distinct section, and each next one takes whichever
  point a quarter, half or three quarters along its own is farthest from the bubbles before it. A route with no
  distinct section, or none as long as 5% of the route, uses its whole length.
- The routes of one `calculateRoute` call carry their [`sections.distinct`](../core/routes.mdx#section-types),
  which the module reads. It compares the routes itself only when one lacks them, such as a route you built.
  Show one response's routes, all of them: distinct sections found against other routes than those shown,
  such as a subset or the routes of two calls, can put a bubble on a road they share.
- `routes` is `'all'` by default. `'selected'` keeps the selected route's bubble alone, and `'alternatives'`
  every bubble but that one.
- The bubbles stay where they are when you select another route or change `routes`, so the one a user just
  clicked stays under their pointer. `selectRoute` updates the routes they show on and their duration
  differences.

### Your own text

To write your own text, set `label.title`. A function receives each route's summary and returns its text, again
whenever another route is selected, so it can read `routeState`, and a route it returns an empty string for gets
no bubble:

```javascript
routingModule.updateConfig({
    summaryBubbles: {
        label: {
            title: (summary) => (summary.properties.routeIndex === 0 ? `Fastest, ${summary.properties.formattedDuration}` : ''),
        },
    },
});
```

- The text comes in plain, in the bubble's font and colour. A MapLibre expression works too, such as
  `['get', 'formattedDuration']`, and draws every bubble.
- `RoutingModuleSerializableConfig` keeps an expression title and leaves out a function one.

## Layer overrides

`layers` is keyed by part (`mainLines`, `waypoints`, `sections`, …) and then by layer. Each entry is a partial
MapLibre layer, and its `paint` and `layout` merge property by property onto the layer the rest of the config
built. Section layers are in [Route sections](./routes-sections.mdx#layer-overrides).

```javascript
const routingModule = await RoutingModule.create(map, {
    widthPreset: 's',
    layers: {
        mainLines: {
            routeLine: { paint: { 'line-blur': 1 } } // keeps the 's' width, the colour and the opacity
        }
    }
});
```

- Name only the properties you change. The ones you leave out keep the values `widthPreset`, `widthFactor`,
  `color` and `opacity` set.
- Don't spread `defaultRoutingLayers` into an entry. It holds the layers drawn with the default config, so its
  `line-width` replaces the one your `widthPreset` and `widthFactor` computed. Read it for the layer names and
  their default values.
- For the route's opacity, set `opacity`, or `sections.<type>.opacity` for a section, rather than a
  `line-opacity` override.

## Every setting as data

`routingKnobCatalogue` lists every plain-valued display setting of the module with its kind, its range or
options, and its default where one value holds everywhere. Each id is the setting's path in the config, so a UI
can build its controls from it without a loaded map:

```javascript
import { routingKnobCatalogue } from '@tomtom-org/maps-sdk/map';

const tunnelKnobs = routingKnobCatalogue.filter(({ id }) => id.startsWith('sections.tunnel.'));
// [{ id: 'sections.tunnel.visible', kind: 'toggle', description: '…', default: true }, …,
//  { id: 'sections.tunnel.style', kind: 'enum', description: '…', options: ['halo', 'inline'], default: 'inline' }, …]
```

The route-wide knobs come first, then each [section type](./routes-sections.mdx)'s knobs, where `<type>` stands
for any of `drawnSectionTypes`:

| Id | Kind |
|---|---|
| `visible` | toggle |
| `color` | color |
| `widthPreset`, `waypoints.sizePreset` | enum: `s`, `m` or `l` |
| `widthFactor`, `waypoints.sizeFactor` | factor, 0.5 to 2 |
| `opacity` | number, 0 to 1 |
| `highlight.outline.widthFactor` | factor, 1 to 1.25 |
| `highlight.section.outline.color` | color |
| `highlight.section.outline.widthFactor` | factor, 0.5 to 2 |
| `waypoints.icon.style.fillColor`, `waypoints.icon.style.outlineColor` | color |
| `waypoints.icon.style.outlineOpacity` | number, 0 to 1 |
| `waypoints.icon.start.style.fillColor`, `waypoints.icon.start.style.outlineColor`, `waypoints.icon.middle.style.fillColor`, `waypoints.icon.middle.style.outlineColor`, `waypoints.icon.finish.style.fillColor`, `waypoints.icon.finish.style.outlineColor` | color |
| `waypoints.icon.start.style.outlineOpacity`, `waypoints.icon.middle.style.outlineOpacity`, `waypoints.icon.finish.style.outlineOpacity` | number, 0 to 1 |
| `waypoints.stopNumbering` | enum: `numbers`, `letters` or `none` |
| `waypoints.highlight.sizeFactor` | factor, 1 to 1.5 |
| `waypoints.label.visible` | toggle |
| `waypoints.label.color`, `waypoints.label.haloColor` | color |
| `waypoints.label.size` | number, 6 to 48 pixels |
| `waypoints.label.haloWidth` | number, 0 to 10 pixels |
| `waypoints.label.opacity` | number, 0 to 1 |
| `waypoints.label.font` | enums: `mapFonts` |
| `waypoints.label.typeface` | enum: `mapTypefaces`, following the map's typeface by default |
| `waypoints.entryPoints` | enum: `main-when-available` or `ignore` |
| `displayUnits.distance.type` | enum: `metric`, `imperial_us` or `imperial_uk` |
| `displayUnits.distance.kilometers`, `displayUnits.distance.meters`, `displayUnits.distance.miles`, `displayUnits.distance.feet`, `displayUnits.distance.yards` | text |
| `displayUnits.time.hours`, `displayUnits.time.minutes` | text |
| `summaryBubbles.visible` | toggle |
| `summaryBubbles.routes` | enum: `all`, `selected` and `alternatives` |
| `summaryBubbles.fields` | enums: `duration`, `distance`, `durationDifference` and `trafficDelay` |
| `summaryBubbles.minZoom`, `summaryBubbles.maxZoom` | number, 0 to 22 |
| `summaryBubbles.color`, `summaryBubbles.label.color` | color |
| `summaryBubbles.label.size` | number, 6 to 48 pixels |
| `summaryBubbles.label.opacity` | number, 0 to 1 |
| `summaryBubbles.label.typeface` | enum: `mapTypefaces`, the map's typeface unset |
| `countryCrossings.visible` | toggle |
| `countryCrossings.minZoom` | number, 0 to 22 |
| `countryCrossings.color`, `countryCrossings.label.color` | color |
| `sections.<type>.visible` | toggle |
| `sections.<type>.color` | color |
| `sections.<type>.opacity` | number, 0 to 1 |
| `sections.<type>.widthPreset` | enum: `s`, `m` or `l` |
| `sections.<type>.widthFactor` | factor, 0.5 to 2 |
| `sections.<type>.style`, `sections.<type>.pattern` | enum |
| `sections.<type>.icon.image` | image |
| `sections.<type>.icon.placement` | enum |
| `sections.<type>.icon.sizeFactor` | factor, 0.25 to 2 |
| `sections.speedLimit.sign.placement`, `sections.speedLimit.sign.priority`, `sections.speedLimit.sign.unit` | enum |
| `sections.speedLimit.sign.minZoom` | number, 0 to 22 |
| `sections.curve.kerb.visible` | toggle |
| `sections.curve.kerb.color` | color |

- A section type lists only the knobs it supports. `speedLimit` takes only `visible` and `sign`.
  `traffic`, which colours its line by delay, has no `color`, `pattern` or `icon` entry. `curve` has a `kerb`
  in place of a `pattern`.
- `routingKnobIds` lists the ids in catalogue order. `RoutingKnobId` is their union, and
  `RoutingKnobValueOf<ID>` is the plain value one takes.
- The unit labels after distances and durations are `text` entries with no default. A `displayUnits` knob left
  unset takes its value from the [global configuration](../core/global-config.mdx).
- The catalogue leaves out `layers`, `beforeLayerConfig`, the waypoint images and `mapping`, the
  `title` of waypoint and bubble labels, and their `customTypeface`, which take structured, MapLibre or function
  values. It leaves out `events` too.
- Its entries share their shape, `KnobEntry`, with every [module's catalogue](./modules.mdx#every-setting-as-data).

## Layer positioning

By default, the route lines sit below the map's labels and the route icons above them. `beforeLayerConfig`
moves them. It takes one target for the whole route (`'top'` or a key of `mapStyleLayerIDs`), or a record keyed
by part, plus `all` for the parts it leaves out:

- `mainLines` - The route lines, and every section line drawn with them.
- `instructionLines` - The guidance lines and their arrows, above the route lines by default.
- `icons` - Waypoints, summary bubbles, border crossings and section icons. The tunnel sign and
  icons requested by generated section types sit lowest. A target puts them all beneath it in that order.

```javascript
// Route lines under the road network; instruction lines and icons stay where they are
routingModule.updateConfig({ beforeLayerConfig: { mainLines: 'lowestRoadLine' } });
```

- Each part keeps its own layers in order wherever it goes. `applyConfig` and `updateConfig` restack them at
  runtime.
- A target the style lacks (the satellite style has no `lowestRoadLine`) puts the part on top. A missing `lowest3D` goes under the POIs first.
- `getLayerToRenderLinesUnder()` follows `mainLines`, and returns `undefined` when the lines are on top.
- The speed limit signs are not a part. Their
  [`sign.priority`](./routes-sections.mdx#speed-limits-are-posted-not-banded) decides which symbols they give way
  to, wherever the parts are.
