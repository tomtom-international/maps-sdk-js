For what the [Styling module](./styling.mdx)'s knobs do not cover, `styling.layers` edits the loaded style's layers directly. It is the advanced, version-coupled tier: prefer a knob or [`setMapColors`](./styling-map-colors.mdx) wherever one covers the intent.

## The advanced tier: `styling.layers`

`styling.layers.query(...)` selects style layers by the base-map group taxonomy, layer type, source layer or id fragment — every constraint given must hold, so the others narrow a `group` — and edits their paint, layout, filter or visibility directly:

```javascript
styling.layers.query({ group: 'roadLabels' }).setPaint({ 'text-color': '#93c5fd' });
styling.layers.query({ metadataGroups: ['water'], layerTypes: ['fill'] }).setPaint({ 'fill-opacity': 0.6 });
styling.layers.query({ idIncludes: ['railway'] }).setVisible(false);
styling.layers.query({ group: 'trafficFlow', layerTypes: ['line'] }).setPaint({ 'line-opacity': 0.6 });
styling.layers.query({ group: 'roadLabels' }).reset();
```

A `group` is either a base-map group, the same names [`BaseMapModule`](./base-map.mdx) takes, or the layers of another style-owned module: `pois`, `trafficFlow`, `trafficIncidents` and `hillshade`. One query covers every style layer, whichever module shows or hides it.

## Only the style's own layers

`styling.layers` and the knobs reach the layers the loaded style declares, nothing added on top of it. The layers a data-owned module adds take their overrides through that module's own `layers` configuration, merged over its defaults and kept across style switches:

| Module | Its `layers` entries |
|---|---|
| [Places](./places-styling.mdx) | `main`, `micro`, `selected`, the cluster layers, and its connections' and entry points' own |
| [Routing](./routes-styling.mdx) | the route lines, waypoints, sections and their labels, by group (`mainLines`, `sections`, …) |
| [Geometries](./geometries.mdx), [Reachable ranges](./reachable-ranges.mdx) | `fill`, `line`, `label`, `lineLabel` |
| [Traffic incident details](./traffic-incident-details.mdx) | the incident lines, from `focusHalo` to `innerPattern`, and the three markers |
| [Traffic area analytics](./traffic-area-analytics-module.mdx) | `heatmap`, each grid's fill, outline, extrusion and highlight, and the region |

The layers you add through `map.mapLibreMap` are yours to style.

The two reach together: below, one switch fades the traffic flow, POIs and road labels through `styling.layers` groups, and brings a city's incidents and boundary forward through their modules' `layers` configs.

## What an edit keeps

- **Re-applied after a style switch** for as long as the layers still exist, and landing on top of the knobs.
- **`setFilter` replaces the filter the style gave a layer**, and the filters other modules add, such as POI categories, still narrow it.
- **Not part of `getConfig()`**: edits are edits to this style, not portable settings.

## How durable is it?

This is the version-coupled tier, framed the way Map Maker frames [raw layer editing](https://docs.tomtom.com/map-maker/documentation/layers/layers): layer ids, property expressions and even which layers exist belong to the style version the map loads. A query that matches nothing warns once and does nothing.

`map.mapLibreMap` remains available below all of this ([which to use when](./about-maplibre.mdx#when-to-use-tomtom-sdk-vs-direct-maplibre)), though `setPaintProperty` on a style layer there is not re-applied after a style switch.
