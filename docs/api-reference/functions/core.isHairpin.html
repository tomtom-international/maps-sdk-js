    Function isHairpin

    • isHairpin(
          curve: Pick<
              CurveSectionProps,
              "headingChangeInDegrees"

              | "maneuver",

      Whether a curve is a hairpin: a bend of the road turning 140° or more on a radius of 40 m or less, as the numbered bends of a mountain pass do.

      Parameters

      • curve: Pick<
            CurveSectionProps,
            "headingChangeInDegrees"
            | "radiusInMeters"
            | "maneuver",
        >

      Returns boolean

      Remarks

      • A wider bend that turns as far, as where a valley road swings round a spur, is a horseshoe a driver takes at speed, and no hairpin.
      • A curve at a maneuver, as a roundabout or a turn at a junction, is no hairpin however far it turns.
      • A curvy stretch's hairpinCount counts its curves this says yes to.

      Example

      const hairpins = route.properties.sections.curve?.filter(isHairpin) ?? [];
